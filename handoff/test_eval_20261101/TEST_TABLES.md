## Limitations

- DEV (full DEV) was used heavily during development of all these lines (method design, critic selection, matrix decisions); only the checkpoint choice within each run is the pre-registered DEV rule. This is the first one-shot TEST read of these DEV-selected checkpoints. Flickr30K TEST was read once before in earlier V9-era work (e.g. job 1042195, full Flickr TEST; 1043800 a 100-image subset), on different models.
- Training GPU types differ between runs and lines (RTX PRO 6000 / L40S / H100 / runfill V100/A100/A40, see the per-unit table); the evaluation jobs ran on runfill GPUs (V100/L40S/A100/A40); the DEV gate bounds evaluation-side differences only.
- Seeds: many groups have one seed; means/stds over three seeds are descriptive, not significance tests. Rankings mix groups with different numbers of seeds.
- Literature rows are copied from the IN2R paper (arXiv:2606.04061v1, Tables 1-2); they use their own noise draws and runs and were not reproduced here. Our noise maps are the locked R25 maps (40%/60%/0%); CC152K noise is real.
- R27-O / R27-O3 use ground-truth oracle rescue/targets during training (diagnostic, not a method). R30 stage paths stop at 10 co-train epochs by design (marked "epochs 10").
- TEST access log: 13 test_read entries (one per evaluation job start, including re-starts after preemption).
