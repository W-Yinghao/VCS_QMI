# Oracle-critic resolution of dependence strength (bivariate Gaussian, correlation rho)

delta_rho = SD(estimate) / |dE[estimate]/drho|, n = 10000 joint pairs. Invariant to monotone
reparametrisation, so estimators with different targets (S, JS, Shannon MI) share one axis.
Ratios > 1 mean VCS resolves rho more finely. Oracle critics only: no approximation or optimisation error.

Readings: VCS beats MINE/NWJ-style single-negative estimators by a margin growing roughly like e^{I/2};
JS (balanced logistic, same equal-mixture structure) matches or beats VCS; InfoNCE with in-batch negatives
beats VCS until I exceeds log N by about 2 nats. Boundedness costs efficiency on clean data.
Scripts: oracle_resolution.py, inbatch_resolution.py (numpy only).
