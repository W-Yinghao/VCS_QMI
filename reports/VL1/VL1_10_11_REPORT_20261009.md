## 3. What this establishes for the paper
- On a real region–phrase relation, the VCS critic is a **calibrated estimator that also ranks**.  Its common J grows with data (4.70 → 6.27 →
  7.20 ×100), and its ranking improves over the frozen CLIP ranking at every N, by +2.8 points at N all.  Matched JS gives the same result
  within 0.5 point, which is the same-posterior control behaving as the theory says.
- The O1 prediction that a calibrated critic plus the known region prior gives a better ranking than the critic alone is confirmed on real data
  (+0.6 point, three routes).  This is a test CIFAR cannot provide.
- Two scorer lessons: (i) the identity-initialised residual scorer matters, because the package's concat scorer never beat raw CLIP; (ii) pair-wise
  sigmoid learning with the actual positive rate (SigLIP-style) is a poor estimator of the balanced target.
- Limits: n = 3 seeds; DEV is a development split.  The softmax and task-selected rows are optimistic.  Real images carry no oracle, so J is a lower
  reading.  Official val / test numbers come only in the final pass.
