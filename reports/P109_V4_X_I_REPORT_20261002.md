The pre-stated question was: "does G2 reach better downstream quality at a lower training score, while an independent refit still recovers a strong pairing?" The table answers it.
- **Yes, for G2.** Its fixed critic scores 0.859 on the same pairs. An independent refit recovers 0.933 on z and 0.945 on L2(h).

## 3. I2 — nested critics and the exact product term (pilot, P102 task)

**Frozen rule.** "Error reduced" requires, in both non-saturated cells (colour s 0.1 and blur σ 0.5), against `indep_sampled` with the sampled readout:
1. |R_orth| at least 50 % smaller;
2. fewer nesting violations;
3. a null-cell |ΔJ| floor that is not larger.

| variant | VCS (both readouts) | JS (both readouts) |
|---|---|---|
| indep_exact (exact Q term only) | no clear reduction | no clear reduction |
| **nested_sampled** | **error reduced** | **error reduced** |
| **nested_exact** | **error reduced** | **error reduced** |

Example, colour s 0.1, VCS, sampled readout:

| variant | R_orth | nesting violations | ΔJ |
|---|---|---|---|
| indep | 0.0367 | 1.4 | 0.0084 |
| nested_sampled | 0.0041 | 0.0 | 0.0026 |
| nested_exact | 0.0064 | 0.0 | 0.0083 |

In the null cell, ΔJ is 0.0031 for indep and 0.0005 / 0.0001 for nested_sampled / nested_exact.

- **Exact enumeration of the product term alone does not reduce the error.** R_orth moves by only 10–25 %.
- **Nesting does.** Per the prereg, this is a statement about **fit quality**: a nested fine critic contains the coarse model by construction. It is not a validation of the L² geometry, and R_orth ≈ 0 is not claimed as such.
- **Caveat on nested_sampled.** It also shrinks the signal: ΔJ falls from 0.0084 to 0.0026 at colour s 0.1. nested_exact keeps the increment (0.0083) while cutting R_orth 6×.
  - nested_exact is therefore the variant a broader measurement would use. That measurement needs a separate prereg, per the rule.
