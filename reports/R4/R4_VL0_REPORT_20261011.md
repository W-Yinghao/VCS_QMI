## 2. Readings (controlled diagnostics, not a causal decomposition)
1. **Box quality is not the bottleneck.**  Replacing the annotated target box by the best detected box costs 0.6–1.1 point for every objective (A → B).
2. **Distractor type at matched count is not the bottleneck either.**  With as many detected distractors as annotated ones, accuracy is *higher* than
   with the annotated same-scene objects (A+D 89–90 vs A+C 85–87).  In that setting VCS ranks better than softmax on both datasets (A+D +1.04 /
   +0.59, B+D +0.74 / +0.57, intervals exclude 0).
3. **The VCS − softmax difference falls with the number of candidates on both datasets.**
   - RefCOCO+: VCS leads with 1–7 distractors (+1.80, +2.08, +0.78), trails from 15 on (−1.02 strict) and most with all of them (−2.87).
   - RefCOCOg: no significant difference up to 7 (+0.41 to +0.06), then softmax leads (−1.67 at 31 strict, −1.48 with all).
   - Softmax-trained encoders keep the largest best-valid − best-invalid margin, VCS the smallest, JS in between, on both datasets.  This is
     **consistent with** an extreme-value contest over many candidates, **but not an explanation**: on RefCOCO+ JS's larger margin than VCS
     (−0.14 [−0.20, −0.08]) comes with the same accuracy (+0.01).
4. **What wins the contest:** a part or duplicate box of the target (22–25 % of queries), then same-category other objects (7–10 %).  On
   RefCOCO+ VCS makes more of both kinds of error than softmax (+2.66 and +1.80 points of queries) and fewer no-matching-annotation errors
   (−1.92).  Training only ever showed whole referred objects as candidates.
5. **Deployable lead (exploratory):** ranking only the top-8 proposals by detector score (query-independent) raises every objective by 6–13 points
   over all proposals, despite lower coverage.
   - At top-4 / top-8, VCS ≥ softmax on RefCOCO+ (+0.71 / +0.86, intervals exclude 0; one top-8 seed is −0.03).
   - On RefCOCOg they tie (+0.26 / +0.05).
   - K was read on DEV here, so a deployable claim needs K chosen on CAL in a new protocol (the R4-VL1 trainer records it).

## 3. Consequence for R4-VL1 (proposal-aware fine-tuning)
- The diagnostics point to the many-candidate regime, where part / duplicate and same-category boxes win, not to box quality.  Training with all
  FIT proposals under the proposal-reference Q_ν (docs/04 §4) targets exactly that, for all three objectives alike.
- A cheaper, deployable comparison arm follows from item 5: detector-score top-K preselection with K fixed on CAL, applied to every objective, with
  no training.  It is recorded by the R4-VL1 trainer for every run.
