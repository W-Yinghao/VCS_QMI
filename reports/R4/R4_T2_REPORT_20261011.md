### 2.2 J(T) versus S (H7)
- **Strictly informative critics (J < S):** the jackknife LCB of J(T) is a valid one-sided bound for S.  With the prescribed-error critic,
  one-sided coverage of S is 97–100 % (the gap S − J = E_M(T − η)² gives slack).
- **The optimum critic (J = S):** coverage of S equals the one-sided coverage of J, 89–96 %.  It is slightly low where the two-sided jackknife is
  low (strong dependence, small n).
- **Not a two-sided S interval:** an interval for J(T) is never reported as a two-sided interval for S.
- **Bounded LCBs:** the max(0, LCB) of the bounded radii is valid but certifies S > 0 only for strong dependence at n = 512 (radius
  √(32 log 20/512) = 0.43).  The weak level (S ≈ 0.05) is never certified at n ≤ 512.

## 3. Research sentences that change
1. **Standard errors (H2, practical).**  Any SE computed as if the n(n − 1) cross pairs, or an image's pooled pairs, were independent is invalid
   in both directions.  Cross-pair estimates need the U-statistic jackknife over base observations (or image-level resampling for image-local
   readouts), not a pair-level SE.  Checked for the existing intervals:
   • P85 / P151 posterior readouts compute J and the posterior MSE on independent EVAL P and Q samples (no shared base observations), so their
     separate unit bootstrap is valid (`src/vcs_estim/benchmark.py` posterior_readouts / boot_units).
   • Their in-batch native readouts resample whole 1 024-anchor blocks.
   • R4-VL0 / VL1 diagnostics resample images.
   This row is prevention, not a correction of a reported interval.
2. **Finite-sample bounds (H4).**  The bounded-difference and Hoeffding radii are valid but 10–44× wider than the asymptotic intervals.  At
   n ≤ 512 they cannot certify weak dependence.  They support only conservative checks, never efficiency claims.
3. **Sparse pairs (H5).**  K = 16 cyclic shifts keep the variance within 1–13 % of the all-pairs estimator at 3.3 % of the critic evaluations
   (n = 512).  K = 1 costs 1.5–2.7× the variance.  This is consistent with P138 and not a new result.
4. **Training vs evaluation (H8).**  Fixed-critic intervals describe EVAL sampling only.  Training variability is negligible at n ≤ 512 but
   dominates at n ≳ 6–10 k, so repo seed spreads at EVAL 32 768 reflect training, not evaluation noise.
