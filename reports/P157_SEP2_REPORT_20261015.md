Q1 < 0 and Q2 < 0 under Holm, so the protocol's CONFIRMED branch applies, and only its permitted sentence may be written:

> **In parametric critic classes under mixture contamination, the quadratic (Brier) rule estimates the posterior more accurately than matched logistic,
> also at four times the data.**

As the protocol requires, this is reported as **the L² projection property of the Brier score that VCS inherits — not as a property of the CS
divergence** — and it stands **next to P156's P3 null at ε = 0**: in the same parametric classes without contamination, VCS and JS are equal (cos-2) or
the comparison is uninformative (P156 §3; `P156_SEP_REPORT_20261015.md`).  The effect is a contamination effect of the scoring rule, not a general
accuracy advantage.

## 2. Secondary readouts (pre-registered)
- **Per-cell absolute Δe, Holm over the 64 cells:** Δe < 0 in **64 / 64** cells and Holm-significant in **64 / 64** (largest p_Holm 2.1e-4).  Absolute Δe from
  −0.0031 (cos-2, false negatives, ε 0.1, d 20, I 4, N 16,384) to −0.0487 (quad-3, independent pairs, ε 0.1, d 20, I 8, N 16,384); relative reduction

- **Common-J selection (sensitivity):** outcome also CONFIRMED (Q1 −0.140 [−0.146, −0.135], Q2 −0.146 [−0.151, −0.141], Q3 −0.089 [−0.094, −0.085], all

  direction does not depend on the selection criterion; the size does.

## 3. Descriptive (not tested)
- **Where the effect is large** (mean relative effect over the 64 S2 cells, native selection): ε 0.1 −0.41 vs ε 0.3 −0.12; independent-pair
  contamination −0.32 vs false negatives −0.21; quad-3 −0.33 vs cos-2 −0.20; I 8 −0.29 vs I 4 −0.25; d 64 −0.28 vs d 20 −0.25.  The combination
  independent pairs × ε 0.1 averages −0.51 (quad-3 up to −0.74); false negatives × ε 0.3 averages −0.11.  Reading: the gap is widest when the
  contamination is a small fraction of structurally different pairs and narrows as ε grows (both rules then fit the contaminated posterior worse).
- **Persistence with N:** across the 32 configurations the relative effect is −0.261 at N 4,096 and −0.275 at N 16,384; it is larger in magnitude at
  the larger N in 28 / 32 configurations — consistent with a population (projection) effect rather than a finite-sample one.
- **Population block S2P** (N 131,072, 8,000 updates, one seed; descriptive): Δe < 0 in **32 / 32** configurations, with values matching the S2 means
  (e.g. quad-3 independent ε 0.1 d 20 I 8: −0.0485 vs −0.0487 at N 16,384).

## 4. QC and limitations

  low edge was never selected; not-converged selections 13–19 of 256 per arm.  No class exceeds the protocol's 50 % threshold, so no limitation is

- The population block is one seed and is not tested.  Seeds 200–207 are fresh; the three gate smoke cells (seed 200) were recomputed in the formal run.
- Both arms start from a = b = c = 0 and use the same lr grid, selection data and budget; the comparison is of scoring rules within a fixed class.

## 5. Not claimed (protocol §4)
Nothing about SSL, VL or natural features; nothing about expressive classes (P156: JS ≥ VCS there in most cells); no robustness statement; no
statement at ε = 0 beyond P156's null; no novelty — the L² projection of the Brier score and the Beta family of proper scoring rules (Buja, Stuetzle &
Shen 2005) are prior art, and P156 §6 lists the noisy-label literature.

## 6. Place in the evidence
This is the first pre-registered, fresh-seed confirmation of a VCS-over-JS accuracy difference in the programme (project map §2 lists none on natural
data).  Its scope is exactly the frozen sentence: parametric critic classes, mixture contamination, posterior accuracy against the contaminated
posterior.  The paper may use it as a property of the quadratic scoring rule (with the ε = 0 null beside it); it does not change any SSL, VL or
estimator-benchmark conclusion.
