## 3. What this means (my reading, under-claimed)
- **The CIFAR result does not transfer to ImageNet-100 at this recipe and budget.**  On CIFAR-10 / 100 (R18 and R50) the balanced-posterior
  objectives match or beat SimCLR on linear; here SimCLR is 3 points ahead after 200 epochs, and its kNN lead (5.3) is larger still.
- Candidate reasons, none tested: (i) the shared recipe was selected on CIFAR, and the VCS family's settings (critic scalars, κ, lr / wd) were
  tuned there, while SimCLR's NT-Xent is known to work at these settings; (ii) 200 epochs is short, and VCS was still closing the gap (−4.0 at
  50, −3.1 at 200); (iii) 100 classes at 224 px change the pair statistics (more distinct negatives per batch).  No claim is made about which
  applies.
- For the paper: the learning section must state the evidence as **CIFAR-scale (R18, R50)**.  A non-CIFAR sufficiency claim is not supported
  by this pilot; if reported, it is reported as "single-seed ImageNet-100 pilot: SimCLR ahead by 3 points at a shared recipe".  This fits the
  estimator-centred framing (learning is secondary), but it removes the option of a broad "VCS as an SSL objective" claim.
