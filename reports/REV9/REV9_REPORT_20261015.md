## 1. Answer to the frozen question
The question was: "Do the two phase-B mechanisms REV8 measured change what the D1 selectors deliver?" **Yes, and in opposite directions for the two learned objectives.**

| objective (CAL-chosen lr) | I1: critic in eval mode (R8-01, dropout) | I2: I1 + straight-through hard top-k (R8-02, relaxation) |
|---|---|---|
| **VCS** (3e-4) | no effect: 0 of 12 cells Holm-significant | **helps: 9 of 12 positive**, up to +4.28 (TextVQA, budget 144); none negative |
| **logistic** (1e-4) | **helps: 8 of 12 positive**, up to +3.57 (TextVQA, 144); none negative | **hurts: 9 of 12 negative**, down to −3.18 vs frozen and −6.75 vs I1 |
| **direct** (1e-4) | essentially none: 1 of 12, +0.26 (TextVQA, 288) | none: 0 of 12 |

**Mechanism agreement (secondary readout of the gate's soft − hard selection loss).**
- **VCS.** The frozen gap is large at small budgets: −0.057 at 72, −0.036 at 144. I2 halves it (−0.028 / −0.019), and I1 leaves it unchanged. So the hard-selection fix works where REV8 located the relaxation gap.
- **Logistic.** The gap is already small (≈ −0.004). I2 overshoots it (+0.017 at 72) and loses accuracy.
- **Direct.** The gap is ≈ 0, so there is nothing for either intervention to change.

**Confound fixed by the design.** Each objective runs at its CAL-chosen learning rate: VCS 3e-4, the other two 1e-4. REV8 found the relaxation gap material at lr 3e-4. So "VCS responds to I2, logistic to I1" cannot be separated from the learning-rate difference. It is a statement about the frozen units as trained, not about the objectives.

## 2. Against the uniform grid (descriptive; the protocol's reading limit applies)
- **D1 shortfall.** The frozen D1 selectors fall below the uniform grid at small budgets, e.g. VCS at budget 72: −4.59 on GQA, −5.97 on TextVQA.
- **After the matching intervention, much of that shortfall closes:**
  - **VCS-I2:** −2.10 / −1.75 on GQA at 72 / 144, −1.91 / −1.71 on TextVQA, and above the grid from budget 288 on TextVQA and at every budget on ChartQA.
  - **logistic-I1:** above the grid on TextVQA at every budget (+2.01 to +3.31) and on ChartQA (+0.71 to +1.65). On GQA it is level at small budgets (−0.41, −0.05) and above at 288 / 432.
- **Not a superiority claim.** The protocol states that a positive effect is a mechanism finding at this scale, not a claim that the selector beats SparseVLM or the grid. The grid rows here have no paired interval.
- **Reading.** A substantial part of the D1 shortfall comes from phase-B training mechanics (dropout in the critic for logistic; the soft relaxation for VCS at lr 3e-4), not from the objectives' targets.
