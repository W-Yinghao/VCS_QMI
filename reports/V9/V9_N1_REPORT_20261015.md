## [解释]
1. **H1 is not supported in N1** (protocol reading rule: VCS-moment vs BBSE / calibrated EM / kernel methods at equal reference budget).  The expected null
   holds exactly — VCS, BCE and BBSE-soft moments are one estimator — and the label-shift baselines (EM; BBSE-hard and EM under shift) are equal or lower in
   RMSE in every design.  This is package §10 row 1 ("VCS 与 rawscore / BBSE 比例相同"): the quadratic fit changes neither the proportion estimate nor its
   variance beyond what any separating score gives.
2. What the fitted critics do add, on natural features, is a *better score* than the raw cosine (0.018 vs 0.024 at seed 901, n 128) — a non-affine
   reshaping of s by tanh(a s + b) — but the gain is shared by BCE and by the kernel fits.
3. The finite-sample set behaves as the theory contract says: conservative where identified, the full parameter space under weak separation, undefined
   (not computed) when the reference is not the batch's component.  Under shift the point estimates are biased by ≈ 0.1 for every method; the only honest
   output there is "assumption failed", which the multi-score consistency residual can flag but not certify (§04.6).
4. Weights derived from any of the coincident methods are the same weights; the N2 weight files will therefore differ between VCS-Weight and BCE-Weight only
   through the fitted critic, not through the proportion step.
