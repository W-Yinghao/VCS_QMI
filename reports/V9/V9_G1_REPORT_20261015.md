## [解释] (frozen wording rules of the protocol)
1. **Against MI, VCS registers more DEV cores at both training fractions under 20 % mismatched supervision** (+18.0 and +22.5 percentage points,
   detected).  This is a development-validation result on DEV cores that r6 had already read.
2. **Against CoMIR, no detectable difference under the frozen rule** at ε 0.2 (+28.0 and +35.2 points, seed intervals include 0).  CoMIR's large
   seed-to-seed variation under mismatch is itself a reported result; the baseline is not weakened or re-tuned.
3. **VCS ≈ logistic ≈ InfoNCE in every cell**: the advantage over MI, NMI and the kernel route is a learned relational-energy class effect, not a
   property of the quadratic objective.  Neither "VCS-only" nor "the quadratic loss is robust" is supported.
4. **20 % displaced positive correspondences leave the three learned critics' success unchanged within the seed intervals** (changes −2.3 to +2.9
   points); only logistic at fraction 1 shows an interval excluding 0 (+2.9 [+2.0, +3.8], an increase).  CoMIR's mean success falls by 10.2 and 23.3
   points with intervals that include 0.
5. **A quarter of the training cores costs the learned critics at most about 3 points** at < 42 px (VCS 95.9 → 93.6), while CoMIR goes from 84.7
   to 74.3.
