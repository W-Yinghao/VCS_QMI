## 2. Reading
1. **On Qwen the VCS selector is below the uniform grid** wherever a claim is made: 7 of 9 grid contrasts, with ChartQA the largest at −15.5 at f 0.25. Together with LLaVA (D1-FULL: S1 below SparseVLM at L25 / L50 on all three benchmarks), the learned selector does not reach a simple or published baseline on either host.
2. **Against its logistic sibling, no host-independent ordering exists.** On Qwen ChartQA, S1 is below S2 at every fraction (claim). On TextVQA the sign flips between scales: S2 is weak at 10k (32.1 at f 0.125) and gains +28.2 at full data. On GQA the contrast is scale-dependent. LLaVA likewise showed scale-dependence (D1-FULL), so "VCS beats logistic" is not supported.
3. **Against direct risk, the answer depends on the benchmark.** S1 is below S3 on GQA, where S3 is trained on GQA risk. It is above S3 on TextVQA, where S3 loses 13–16 points at full data.
4. **Scale (descriptive).** Full data helps S1 modestly: TextVQA +5.34 at f 0.25 and ChartQA +1.79. It helps S2 a lot on TextVQA, and it hurts S3 on TextVQA.
