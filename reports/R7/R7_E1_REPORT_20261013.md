- The learned selectors' columns (S1–S3) are below the grid column at every fraction on all three benchmarks (36 of 36 cells; R7-40c's own rows).

What this does and does not show.  On this host, two published training-free pruning methods keep much more quality at small budgets than both
the uniform grid and the D1 learned selectors.  E1 differs from R7-40c in more than the selection rule: own pinned environments, one
interpreter per fraction, and each method's own internal mechanics at the same nominal fraction.  So the side-by-side reading is not a
same-condition comparison, and nothing here enters a family or a claim about S1–S3.
