## 3. Reading (convention of this report, not a frozen rule: Holm-adjusted p < 0.05 → "S1 lower / higher"; otherwise "no detectable difference")
- **S1 − S2 (VCS vs logistic, same architecture and recipe): S1 is lower** at L25 and L50 on all three benchmarks (−0.6 to −4.4 points) and at
  L75 on TextVQA (−0.4); no detectable difference at L75 on GQA and ChartQA.  The VCS-trained selector does not beat its logistic sibling; the
  logistic one is better at tight budgets.
- **S1 − S3 (VCS vs direct risk regression): benchmark-dependent.**  S1 is lower on GQA at L25 / L50 (−1.2, −0.6), higher on TextVQA at every
  point (+3.6 to +9.1) and on ChartQA at L25 / L50 (+1.3, +0.8).  The direct-risk selector, trained on GQA binary risk, transfers worst to
  OCR-heavy TextVQA.
- **S1 − SparseVLM (budget-matched): S1 is lower** at L25 and L50 on all three benchmarks (−1.1 to −17.0 points; −17.0 on TextVQA at L25);
  no detectable difference at L75.
- **Secondary, descriptive:** at 12.5–25 % of the tokens S1 is below the training-free uniform grid on all three benchmarks (GQA 77.8 vs 81.1
  and TextVQA 26.6 vs 32.6 at 25 %); S2 is close to the grid.  At 75 %, S1 and S2 are within 0.7 point of full on all three
  benchmarks; S3 is within 0.7 point on GQA and ChartQA but 3.9 points below full on TextVQA (42.01 vs 45.89).

## 4. What this means for D1 (10k-image scale)
- The pre-registered D1 hypothesis — a VCS-trained, question-conditioned risk selector gives a better quality–latency trade-off than its
  logistic and direct-risk siblings and than SparseVLM — **is not supported** on LLaVA-1.5 at this scale: S1 is below S2 and SparseVLM wherever a
  difference is detected, and below the uniform grid at tight budgets.
- The CAL ranking signal was weak (Spearman 0.088 for S1, 0.093 for S2 — equal within seed noise), so the CAL readout did not anticipate the
  S1 − S2 quality gap.  The direct-risk selector's best CAL ranking did not translate into quality on TextVQA (risk targets come from GQA only).
- Per the claim rule, the final D1 statement waits for D1-FULL (selectors retrained on FIT_FULL: 70,256 images — every eligible GQA training image outside CAL and DEV; 74,256 eligible in total).  The official-test read
  (R7-50 phase L) reports the same rows on GQA test-dev and ChartQA test; the Qwen replication is R7-40c.

## 5. Caveats
- Selectors are trained on GQA binary yes / no questions only; TextVQA and ChartQA are transfer settings.
- 3 seeds per selector; intervals: image (chart) grouped paired bootstrap, 2,000 replicates, per seed (frozen); per-seed differences are in the results JSON.
- Latency is the profiling-workload latency at B* = 4 (not each set's mean end-to-end latency); timing ran while the node's other GPU was busy.
- Secondary rows (A1 / A2 ablations, dynamic budgets, DEV risk calibration, per-subset tables) are in the results JSON; none changes the
  primary reading.
