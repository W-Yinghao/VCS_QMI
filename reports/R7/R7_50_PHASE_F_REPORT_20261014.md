  with every seed negative.  The TextVQA claims (S1 above S3) have no official-test counterpart (TextVQA test is not run).
- **S1 − S2 at full scale: no stable difference.**  DEV / val had no claim (−0.06 GQA, −0.10 ChartQA at L25).  On the official test L25 is
  detected in opposite directions (GQA −0.78, ChartQA +0.75), each mostly one seed (GQA seed 2: −2.35; ChartQA seed 0: +2.17; the other
  seeds within ±0.21).  The interval covers image sampling only, not seed variation, so these two cells do not show a family-level difference.
  The 10k deficit of S1 against S2 on the test (phase L: −3.24 GQA, −0.87 ChartQA at L25) is −0.78 on GQA and reversed on ChartQA at full FIT
  (descriptive, F4).
- **S1 − S3 at L25 on GQA (+2.26, detected) is driven by S3 seed 1** (per-seed +0.63, +9.88, −3.72; S3 seed 1 keeps 46.57 at 144 tokens
  against 54.83 and 58.84 for seeds 0 and 2); DEV had +0.38, not detected.  No other S1 − S3 cell is detected.

  of 18; of the five sign flips, four are not detected on either reading and one is ChartQA S1 − S2 at L25 (detected on the test only).
- **Fixed budgets (descriptive):** on GQA test-dev S1 is below the uniform grid at 12.5–50 % (52.84 / 55.73 / 59.23 vs 56.26 / 58.70 / 60.29)

  below it at 50 %.  The S1 dynamic rows are below the fixed rows on GQA at all three operating points (A3, secondary).
