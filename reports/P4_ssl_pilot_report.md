## D. Observations (single seed, 20 epochs: hypotheses, not verdicts)

**Did the critic learn?**  Yes.  Training `J_raw` rose monotonically from 0.17 (epoch 1) to 0.68 (epoch 20) and was still rising
slowly when the LR reached 1 % of base.  On selection images (never used for SSL fitting) `heldout_J` = 0.6766 ± 0.0046 over four
augmentation/shift repeats, with `t_pos` ≈ +0.68 and `t_neg` ≈ −0.68.  The value stayed well below the bound 1, negatives were
saturated (|t| > 0.95) about half the time and positives about 17 % of the time.

**Did the encoder improve?**  Yes, relative to its own random initialization under the identical frozen-probe protocol:
linear-val 41.78 → 56.26 % (+14.5), kNN 36.58 → 41.08 % (+4.5), effective rank of `h` 2.94 → 5.79.  The kNN monitor dipped below the
random-init value at epoch 5 (35.64 %) before recovering.  The improvement is far smaller than that of both matched controls
(+33 linear, +31 kNN), which reached 75.0 % / 75.6 % linear-val at the same budget.

**Train / validation gap?**  Not in the critic objective: epoch-20 training `J_raw` (0.678) and `heldout_J` (0.677) agree within the
repeat SD.  The gap is elsewhere: pair discrimination improved on held-out images (J ≈ 0.68) while frozen-feature quality improved
only modestly.  This must be reported as **"pair discrimination improved without proportional transfer to representation quality"**;
`heldout_J` is a diagnostic of the current critic, not an estimate of the population S and not a certificate.

**Saturation / collapse?**  No operational `COLLAPSE_SUSPECTED` flag (effective rank stayed > 2 at every evaluation, covariance trace
grew).  But the representation concentrated strongly: `z_l2` effective rank fell from 31.2 (random init) to 3.0 by epoch 5 and 4.05 at
epoch 20; `p_raw` has four dominant eigenvalues (389, 319, 249, 222) and then a cliff (3.6); `h` effective rank 5.8 versus 29.1 (SimCLR)
and 24.8 (VICReg); the `h` norm grew from 21.4 to 34.4.  Hypothesis only: with K = 1 and B = 256, the ordered-concat tanh critic can
separate the B positive pairs from the B shifted pairs using a ≈ 4-dimensional projector subspace, so the joint objective has weak
pressure to spread information across dimensions.  Nothing in the pilot tests this; K, critic capacity and training length were not
varied, and no regularizer was added (none is authorized).

**Are the controls normal?**  Yes for a 20-epoch, single-seed CIFAR-10 ResNet-18 pilot: SimCLR-matched 74.98 % / kNN 67.9 %,
VICReg-matched-128 75.58 % / kNN 68.2 %, smooth probe curves, ranks 25–29, losses decreasing as expected.  These are component-matched
short runs, not tuned native baselines; no ranking claim between VCS and the controls is made from one seed.

**Probe protocol behaviour (reported for all methods, not adjusted).**  With un-normalized `h` (norm 21–34) and SGD lr 0.1 the probe
is noisy in its first ≈ 40 epochs (random-init curve 23–42 %, VCS epoch-20 curve 39.7–56.3 %), converging under the cosine decay;
SimCLR/VICReg curves are smooth.  The same `initial.pt` probed on the A100 vs the RTX PRO 6000 gave 41.78 vs 41.94 % — a ≈ 0.2-point
hardware/float-noise floor on this metric.  The final-probe-epoch rule was applied uniformly; no probe search was done for any method.

**Reproducibility.**  CPU single-worker resume is bit-exact (test 7).  On GPU, two *identical* continuous 100-step runs differ as much
as the interrupted+resumed pair (max |Δ| params 4.1e-2 vs 4.3e-2; |Δ loss| 0.12 vs 0.08; pair shifts identical), so the resume difference
is cuDNN run-to-run nondeterminism amplified by AdamW, not a checkpoint bug.  Consequence: 20-epoch endpoints carry unquantified
run-to-run noise (`torch.use_deterministic_algorithms` was not enabled, per the FP32-reference scope) — a reason for multi-seed
confirmation, not a reason to change the method.

**Where the cost is.**  Per step the critic adds ≈ 5 % (same-GPU smoke); the dominant costs are the encoder forward/backward and, for
VCS, the optional hold-out diagnostics (4 repeats × 4 epochs ≈ 82 s).  Memory ≈ 2.8 GB allocated at B = 256 on either GPU.

## E. Next step (one prioritized proposal; nothing is started)

No implementation error was found (all gates PASS; `J = 1 − R`, gradient routing, RNG isolation, split isolation and test-set
inaccessibility verified on real data).  Following the pre-registration, the proposed next round is the **200-epoch confirmation
from the same initial weights, warm-up 10 epochs, seeds {0,1,2}, all three methods, otherwise unchanged recipe**
(`configs/next_stage_plan.yaml`).  It answers the two open questions the pilot cannot: whether the low-rank VCS representation and
the slowly rising J persist or resolve under the full schedule, and how large single-seed noise is.  Measured cost: ≈ 105 min per
VCS seed on the A100-PCIE (≈ 75 min per control seed on the RTX PRO 6000), ≈ 13 GPU-hours total if run one job at a time.

configs, after this decision) are K (the pairing reference already supports K > 1) or critic capacity.  CIFAR-100, ImageNet, EEG,
image–text, and any regularizer/EMA/queue are out of scope for this decision.
