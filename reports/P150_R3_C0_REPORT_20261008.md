## 2. Reading (frozen §4)
**(a) holds — the condition supports detection only.**  No critic's common J is clearly positive at h (all intervals at or below 0), while the
colour cell is rejected in 0.60 / 0.55 / 0.30 / 0.30 of draws at h against 0.05 in the null.  On the common scale the planted attribute moves J
by at most 0.002 (colour − null; S_plugin colour − null ≤ 0.001) — the size of the per-draw bootstrap half-width (median 0.0002–0.0034 by critic)
and smaller than the held-out fit offset that every critic shows in the null cell (J −0.0004 to −0.0075).  **(b) does not hold**: the audit class is not clearly positive
either, so the P144 reading (J at h ≤ 0) was not a function-class limitation of the nested critic — the P143 class at the same n gives the same
J.  **(c) cannot be read**: J is not positive at h, so no h → O loss is quantified; detection itself drops from 0.60 at h to 0.15 at O (null 0.00
at O for the audit class; 3 / 20 draws, not distinguishable from α at this n).

## 3. What this means for the paper (owner)
The P143 table is a **detection** table: a permutation test of a fitted critic's own statistic can be powerful (0.60) where the estimated
dependence on the S / J scale is indistinguishable from zero (S of order 1e-3).  Detection power and estimable dependence must not be described as
the same quantity, and the P144 statement "J_h ≤ 0 at n 3 000" is the expected reading of an S of order 1e-3, not a failure of the nested estimator.
Not claimed: any value of S for the planted attribute; other encoders, sites or colour strengths; larger n.
