## 1. Truth-compression check (Gaussian chain B(64) ⊇ M(8) ⊇ A(4); true B→M increment 0, true M→A 0.017 (weak) / 0.044 (moderate))

**Frozen 4-criterion rule** (vs indep_sampled / sampled readout = the P102 baseline, both non-null conditions): **only VCS nested_exact with the sampled
readout is "error reduced"**; every other variant is "no clear reduction"; indep_sampled-with-exact-readout and the constant-zero critic are "shrinkage"
(the zero control fails as designed).

| moderate condition (true M→A 0.0441) | err B→M (truth 0) | err M→A (RMSE) | err B→A | posterior MSE at B / A | R_orth |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| VCS indep_sampled (baseline) | −0.0204 | −0.0103 (0.0105) | −0.0306 | 0.0433 / 0.0123 | +0.042 |
| **VCS nested_exact, sampled readout** | **−0.0001** | −0.0069 (**0.0070**, 0.67×) | **−0.0069** | **0.0157** / 0.0073 | +0.0002 |
| JS indep_sampled | −0.0192 | −0.0109 (0.0109) | −0.0301 | 0.0442 / 0.0132 | +0.051 |
| JS nested_exact, sampled readout | +0.0000 | −0.0085 (0.0087, 0.80×) | −0.0085 | 0.0171 / 0.0078 | 0.0000 |

- The large gain is at the **zero-increment step**: independent fitting reads a spurious B→M "increment" of −0.020 (the 64-d critic under-fits relative to
  the 8-d one), nested_exact removes it (−0.0001), and the posterior error at B drops ≈ 2.8×; the total B→A error falls from −0.031 to −0.007.
- The positive increment M→A is still under-read by all variants (≈ −0.005 to −0.009), but **not shrunk to zero** (criterion 4 holds for nested_exact:
  |ΔJ| ≥ 0.5 × truth), so the low R_orth is not mere shrinkage — unlike the zero critic.
- JS nested_exact misses the rule only on criterion 1 in the moderate condition (M→A RMSE 0.80× vs the 0.75× bound); its zero-increment behaviour is as good.
- Null condition: all variants have increment errors ≤ 0.001 (no false increments).

## 3. Reading
- The P109 pilot's suggestion holds up against a known truth: **nested_exact removes the spurious increment between nested sets and lowers posterior error,
  without merely shrinking real increments** — for VCS by the full rule, for JS on 3 of 4 criteria.  Independent fitting's main failure is exactly the
  under-fit of the larger set (a false negative "increment" at a true-zero step).
- Remaining limitation: real positive increments are still under-estimated by ≈ 20–30 % in every variant (finite fitting).
- Not claimed: anything beyond this Gaussian chain and these two visual conditions; nested R_orth ≈ 0 is fit quality by construction, not a validation of
  representation geometry.
