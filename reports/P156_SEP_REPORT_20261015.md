## 5. Consequences and proposals (nothing below is run)
**For the ledger and the paper.**
- VCS / JS stays "same posterior, different scoring rule".
- **May say.**
  - The P1 sentence above, together with the budget qualification (at 4 × the data the logistic lead grows under contamination).
  - The γ-axis facts as description.
- **May not say.**
  - Any "better than JS" sentence based on the Brier projection at ε = 0 (P3 falsifier).
  - "Robust to contamination".
  - Anything about SSL, VL or image features (protocol, "Not claimed").

**Proposed confirmatory unit (owner decision).**
- **Primary:** Δe in parametric critic classes (cos-2, the application form, and quad-3) under independent-pair and false-negative contamination.
- **Seeds:** fresh, 100+.
- **Budget scaling:** N 4,096 and 16,384, to separate a population projection effect from a finite-sample one.
- **lr grid:** widened to 1.0.
- **Mechanism check first:** large-sample (200 k) population fits of both rules in these classes, on CPU.
- If it holds, it is the one region where the quadratic rule is the more accurate posterior estimator, and it is the critic form the applications use.

**Follow-up (2026-10-15).** The proposed confirmatory unit ran as P157 (`P157_SEP2_PROTOCOL_FROZEN_20261015.md`, fresh seeds 200–207): outcome CONFIRMED (persists at 4× N) — see `P157_SEP2_REPORT_20261015.md`; the ε = 0 null of §3 stands unchanged.
