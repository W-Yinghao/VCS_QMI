## 5. Execution notes (ambiguities resolved by the V8-eval fork at implementation; not in the frozen YAML)
1. "Normalised RPS" = the mean over the four thresholds of the natural Brier (the U-RPS risk form), reported next to the per-threshold values.
2. Mean-rating MSE / MAE reported both prompt-macro (the CAL_SELECT unit) and natural-weighted.
3. CAL_PROB temperature = one scalar on the 5 logits from a fixed log grid [0.25, 4] minimising natural-weighted histogram log loss; secondary column only.
4. The common-defined-subset sensitivity is V7X-50's, not pre-specified in V8-03 → labelled as a sensitivity.
5. Table C kernel routes are not scored on GenAI-Rank (not in the V8-03 families); their predictions exist for a later descriptive row if wanted.
6. full_rows (U-CE, U-L1) is a 4-contrast descriptive family with Holm inside it.
