## How to read this register
**Status vocabulary.**
- **clear**: explicit permissive terms (CC0, MIT, Apache-2.0, BSD). Publishing results needs only the customary citation. Licence notices apply only if we redistribute the item or a derivative.
- **attribution-or-notice needed**: explicit terms that require credit or a licence notice for what we publish or share. Examples: CC BY / CC BY-SA data, ODbL, a per-image credit for any dataset image shown, and the GPL or Llama notices if we redistribute.
- **non-commercial**: the terms limit use to non-commercial or research purposes. Academic publication of results fits these terms. Commercial use does not, and neither does sharing derived data outside the terms.
- **unknown**: the official source states no terms, only third parties state any, or the source could not be verified.
- **conflicting**: official or quasi-official sources disagree. The row names both readings and the stricter one.

**"Our authorisation record"** quotes the owner's recorded decision, or the manifest's `auth` field, and says what that record covers. A download instruction is not an acceptance of terms; where the two differ, the row says so. "None recorded" means nothing was found in the repository, its pre-registrations or the provenance files.

**Dataset terms vs image-source terms** are kept apart wherever they differ:
- GQA vs Visual Genome / Flickr;
- COCO annotations vs Flickr images;
- TextVQA and Localized Narratives vs Open Images;
- POPE and M-HalDetect vs COCO val2014;
- AV-MNIST vs MNIST / FSDD / ESC-50.
