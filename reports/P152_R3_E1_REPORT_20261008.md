## 3. A finding not in the panel design: the fixed budget overfits at low dependence for every method
At I 2 the value after 2 000 updates is far below the SELECT-best value for every method (VCS 0.21 vs 0.50; JS −0.22 vs 0.61; InfoNCE −0.49 vs 1.76;
SMILE −0.05 vs 1.67; NWJ 1.36 vs 1.76; DV 1.25 vs 1.77), and posterior MSE rises from 0.065 to 0.357 (VCS) and 0.061 to 0.307 (JS); the SELECT
optimum is at update 100–200 for most fits (up to 900 for one VCS / JS seed and 400–1 500 for NWJ / DV seeds).  At I 6 / 10 VCS, JS and InfoNCE are within 0.05 of their optimum at 2 000, while NWJ and DV are still rising
(selected at updates 1 400–2 000, i.e. budget-limited).  Every estimator's reported value therefore depends on early stopping on a held-out role;
this applies equally to all six methods.

## 4. Reading (frozen §3; no success criterion for VCS)
- On gradient-norm variability **in-batch InfoNCE is the most stable** at every level, matched JS second; VCS is somewhat higher than JS at I 2 / 6 (0.22 vs 0.13, 0.20 vs 0.16)
  and higher at I 10 because of one large-lr seed.  NWJ and DV are 3–19× more variable than InfoNCE at I ≥ 6; corrected SMILE inherits JS's
  gradient but is selected at larger lrs at I 6 / 10 (P85's choice), which raises its variability.
- On evaluation variance the bounded-score methods and InfoNCE are equivalent (relative sd within 0.002–0.004 of each other); NWJ / DV / SMILE are
  1.4–4× higher at I ≥ 6.
- On output distributions VCS is the least saturated, and it is the only objective without massive score overflow on negatives at the registered lr.
- No method failed numerically with in-batch negatives.
The defensible statement for the paper: with in-batch negatives, the bounded objectives (VCS, matched JS) and InfoNCE form a stable group and the
exponential-family estimators (NWJ, DV, SMILE) an unstable one on every recorded field; within the stable group VCS is **not** uniquely the most
stable — its distinctive properties are the shared target S, unsaturated outputs and no score overflow, not lower gradient variance.  (Writing item
W7 stands.)
