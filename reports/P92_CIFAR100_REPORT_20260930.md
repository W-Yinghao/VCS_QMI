## 2. Pre-stated readings
- **(a) Ordering** (CIFAR-10: 2× SimCLR > VICReg > VCS; 8× SimCLR > {VICReg ≈ VCS}).  2× linear: SimCLR − VCS +1.46 (matches), VCS − VICReg **+1.53 (opposite
  sign, ≥ 1.0)**.  8× linear: VCS − SimCLR **+1.66 (opposite sign, ≥ 1.0)**, VCS − VICReg +3.90.  **Verdict by the letter: "does not replicate"** — on
  CIFAR-100 VCS is above VICReg at both budgets and above SimCLR at 8× on the linear probe.  On kNN the CIFAR-10 SimCLR lead replicates at both budgets
  (+5.71, +2.26), and VCS ≈ VICReg at 2× / VCS > VICReg at 8×.
- **(b) Scaling** (CIFAR-10: VCS's 2× → 8× gain +2.5 vs controls +0.5 / +0.4).  CIFAR-100 linear gains: VCS **+1.95**, SimCLR **−1.17**, VICReg −0.42.
  **Verdict by the letter: "scaling replicates"** (VCS's gain exceeds each control's by ≥ 1.0).  kNN gains: VCS +6.28, SimCLR +2.83, VICReg −0.18 — the same
  ordering on the probe-free metric.

## 3. Caveat that must travel with (a) and (b): the SimCLR 8× linear number is probe-limited
SimCLR's 8× linear accuracy is *below* its own 2× value (58.25 vs 59.42) while its kNN keeps rising (55.4 → 57.4 between epochs 200 and 800 for seed 0,
all three seeds alike).  The frozen linear probe (SGD 0.1, 100 epochs, un-normalised h — tuned on CIFAR-10) ends at a training cross-entropy of 1.12 for
SimCLR 8× against 0.89 for SimCLR 2× and 0.86–0.89 for VCS: the probe underfits the 8× SimCLR representation on 100 classes.  VICReg's probe also ends high
(1.18–1.21 at both budgets).  So the linear-probe inversions at 8× (VCS > SimCLR) and the negative SimCLR scaling are at least partly a property of the fixed
probe, not of the representation; the kNN column (no probe optimisation) keeps SimCLR ahead at both budgets.  The prereg forbids re-running with new
settings inside P91; a probe-robustness check (e.g. h normalised or a longer probe, applied identically to all 18 checkpoints) would be a separate,
evaluation-only addendum — listed for the owner, not run.
